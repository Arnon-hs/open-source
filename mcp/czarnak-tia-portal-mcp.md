**Project Summary:**

Czarnak/tia-portal-mcp is an open-source bridge that enables seamless connectivity between Large Language Models (LLMs) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) agents with Siemens SIMATIC TIA Portal V21. This specialized bridge facilitates direct interactions between AI assistants and real tools, standardizing integrations and expediting data exchange. By leveraging the Model Context Protocol, Czarnak/tia-portal-mcp empowers developers to build more efficient and effective AI-powered workflows.
