### English

Bouncer is an MCP proxy that blocks sends to addresses already read by the agent, simplifying the connection of AI assistants to real tools via a standard protocol. Deploy the Bouncer server, configure its deny rules, and hook it into your existing MCP interface to quickly prototype and integrate internal workflows. It’s production‑ready for environments needing rapid communication control, though license and maintenance checks are advised before scaling.
