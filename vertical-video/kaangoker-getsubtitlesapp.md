> A user-friendly desktop app to automatically generate accurate .srt subtitles for video or audio files. Powered by OpenAI's Whisper, it supports 99 languages and can also translate subtitles into English. It features a special mode for vertical videos (Reels, Shorts, TikTok) and can be run easily via desktop app or Docker.
