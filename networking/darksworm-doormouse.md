## 📝 Summary

### English

Doormouse is a reverse proxy that wakes sleeping servers via Wake‑on‑LAN, enabling instant access without manual power‑on. To adopt it, simply insert Doormouse into your routing chain and configure the target machines’ MAC addresses; once compatibility is verified, it’s ready for use. The project is prototype‑grade, but with dependency and maintenance checks it can serve internal workflows.
