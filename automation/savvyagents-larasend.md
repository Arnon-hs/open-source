### English

Savvyagents/larasend is an open-source, self-hosted transactional email platform designed for Laravel, allowing users to send emails through their own AWS SES account while maintaining control over the dashboard, API keys, and delivery logs. By adopting larasend, users can automate repetitive manual operations and connect tools into repeatable flows, streamlining their workflow and increasing efficiency. With its high production readiness, recent activity, and strong ecosystem signals, larasend is well-suited for a serious pilot, offering a straightforward evaluation process and a low-risk adoption path for those looking to integrate a reliable transactional email solution into their infrastructure.
