> VaultExam AI turns confidential Document(Of any type) into page-cited answers and print-ready exam papers with answer keys and Set A/B/C variants. Built for coaching institutes and trainers, it runs fully locally on open-weight models (Ollama + PGVector). No cloud, no data leaks, no per-token fees.
