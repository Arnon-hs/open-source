> A local-first AI assistant built for one real person — my dad. A small open-weight model (Qwen3.5-0.8B via llama.cpp) handles conversation; a deterministic SQLite core — never the model — decides medicine confirmations, email sending, and reminders. Runs offline; his data never leaves the laptop.
