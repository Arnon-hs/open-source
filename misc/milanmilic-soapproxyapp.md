## 📝 Summary

### English

SoapProxyApp is a lightweight HTTP/SOAP API debugger that lets you inspect requests, responses, headers, and payloads in real time. Adopt it by running it locally as a proxy and pointing your client to its URL, then use the captured traffic for debugging or documentation. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but verify dependency health and maintenance before deploying to production.
