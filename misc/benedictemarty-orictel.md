OricTel turns an Oric 1/Atmos into a Minitel 1B terminal that can connect to Téléte servers. To adopt it, hook the device to your network, install the necessary drivers, and configure the connection settings in the provided config file. It’s ready for prototype or internal workflow use, though you should verify dependencies and maintenance before production deployment.
