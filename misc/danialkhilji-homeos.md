HomeOS is a self‑hosted family dashboard for a kitchen touchscreen that centralizes control of household devices and schedules. To adopt, deploy it on your own server, connect the desired sensors via its API, and then integrate with home gadgets. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, though you should audit dependencies and maintenance before production use.
