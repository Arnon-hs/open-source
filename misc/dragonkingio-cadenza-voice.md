> Local-first voice input for macOS (随言). Hold a shortcut, speak, and the text is typed at your cursor. Recognition runs on your Mac with SenseVoice, FireRedASR2 or Parakeet; cloud services are optional, with your own keys and consent. No account, no telemetry. 本地优先的 macOS 语音输入，开源 MIT。
