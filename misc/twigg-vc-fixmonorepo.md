## 📝 Summary

### English

FixMonorepo transforms a monorepo into a set of single-file repositories, simplifying dependency management and speeding up builds. To adopt it, first analyze your project structure, then manually move files into new repos and configure CI/CD pipelines. The tool is suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but you should verify licensing, maintenance, and release cadence before deploying to production.
