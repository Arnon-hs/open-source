### English

Antidraw is an open‑source AI‑powered prototyping canvas that plugs into any React library, letting you quickly sketch, iterate, and export UI components. Start by installing the package, configuring your preferred plugins, and drawing directly in your app, then hand off the finished assets to your workflow. It’s production‑ready at a medium level—actively maintained but still requires a review of dependencies and docs before full deployment.
