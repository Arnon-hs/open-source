## 📝 Summary

### English

Thingport is a self‑hosted web service that consolidates your entire 3D model collection into a single, searchable interface, simplifying management and retrieval. To adopt it, deploy the app on your server, connect your model repositories, and configure API keys, then integrate it into your existing workflows. The project is prototype‑ready: it works well for internal use, but you should audit dependencies and maintain regular updates before moving to production.
