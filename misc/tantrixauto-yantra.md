### English

Yantra is an LALR(1) parser generator for C++ that lets you quickly build efficient parsers for custom languages. To adopt it, simply add the library to your project, include the headers, and generate a parser from a grammar file—documentation and examples guide the setup. Production readiness is medium: it’s ideal for prototypes and internal workflows, but you should verify maintenance, dependencies, and licensing before deploying it in production.
