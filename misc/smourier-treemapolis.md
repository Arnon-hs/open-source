## 📝 Summary

### English

Treemapolis lets you fly through your disks as a city, turning file systems into interactive 3D maps that simplify navigation and discovery. Start by pulling the Docker image, mounting your data volume, and tweaking settings via environment variables. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, yet you should audit dependencies and maintenance before deploying to production.
