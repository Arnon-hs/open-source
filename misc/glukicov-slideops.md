## 📝 Summary

### English

SlideOps automatically updates slides from a repository and flags when they drift from the code, keeping documentation in sync. Start by adding it to your CI/CD pipeline and manually reviewing changes, then enable full automation. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but requires dependency checks and maintenance verification before production use.
