## 📝 Summary

### English

AnywhereDesign is a local‑first voice cockpit for React that lets you add voice commands to your UI with minimal effort. Start by dropping the component into your app, wiring up a microphone, and then extend it with plugins as needed. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but verify updates, dependencies, and documentation before deploying to production.
