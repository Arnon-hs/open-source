## 📝 Summary

### English

Readfirst is a repo map that coding agents read before editing files, improving transparency and reducing the risk of unexpected changes. To adopt it, start with small tests on repos with clear READMEs and activity, then gradually roll out to your internal workflows. Production readiness is medium: suitable for prototypes and internal processes, but verify license, maintenance, docs, issues, and release cadence before full deployment.
