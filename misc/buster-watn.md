### English

Watn lets you ask questions in your shell and receive ready-to-run commands, speeding up problem solving and automation. To adopt, install via npm, run `watn <question>`, and embed the returned command into your scripts. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but verify dependencies and maintenance before production use.
