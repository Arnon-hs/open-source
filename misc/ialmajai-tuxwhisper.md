## 📝 Summary

### English

TuxWhisper is an offline push‑to‑talk dictation tool for Linux that works on Wayland. To adopt it, bind a hotkey in your terminal or desktop environment to start recording, then let the local model transcribe your speech. The project is at a prototype stage but has a recent release and maintenance cadence, making it suitable for internal workflows after verifying dependencies and license.
