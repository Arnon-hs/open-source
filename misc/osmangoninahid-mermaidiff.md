## 📝 Summary

### English

Mermaidiff turns git diffs into sequence diagrams, making PR behavior easier to grasp. Adopt it by installing the package, hooking it into your CI pipeline, and viewing the generated diagrams in pull‑request reviews. It’s production‑ready for prototypes and internal workflows, though you should audit dependencies and maintenance before full deployment.
