### English

Here's a 2-3 sentence summary for the open-source project:

The mit-cml/appinventor-sources project is an open-source implementation of the MIT App Inventor, enabling developers to add AI capabilities to their applications without starting from scratch. Its value lies in streamlining the development of AI features, RAG (Role Assignment Graph) or agent workflows, and model tooling evaluation. While it offers medium production readiness, careful integration, and setup validation are necessary before committing to production use, making it suitable for prototypes or internal workflows.
