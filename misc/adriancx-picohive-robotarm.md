### English

The Raspberry Pi PicoW and LeRobot SO-ARM100 are controlled via a web page, MQTT, and OpenAI, enabling rapid prototyping of autonomous robots. To adopt, connect the device to a network, configure an MQTT broker, and integrate the OpenAI API, then control the arm from any browser. The project is suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but production use requires verifying dependency support and documentation currency.
