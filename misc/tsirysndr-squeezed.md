## 📝 Summary

### English

Squeezed pipes any audio into synchronized Squeezebox players without needing a LMS, simplifying music streaming across home or office networks. To adopt, install the tool, connect the players to the same network, and point it at your audio source—setup takes just a few minutes. The project is prototype‑ready, suitable for internal workflows, but requires monitoring of dependencies and maintenance before full production use.
