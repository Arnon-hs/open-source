### English

Dialtrace visualizes where time is spent during a voice AI call, simplifying performance analysis and bottleneck detection. To adopt, integrate its SDK into your phone system, enable metric collection, and feed the data into your monitoring dashboards. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but production use requires checking dependency health and maintenance status.
