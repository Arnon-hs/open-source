## 📝 Summary

### English

Zebkit lets you embed Chromium’s engine as a library you link into your application rather than running a separate runtime. It’s ideal for prototypes and internal workflows: add the dependency, hook up the API, and verify compatibility with your environment. Currently the project is prototype‑ready, so before production use you should audit its activity, documentation, and update cadence.
