## 📝 Summary

### English

NostrComments lets you create comment threads for any URL, storing them on public relays. To adopt, simply connect a client to a relay and add a “Comment” button to your page. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, though you should audit dependencies and documentation before production use.
