## 📝 Summary

### English

Rails-preflight is a static checker that flags blockers to your next Rails upgrade, letting you spot dependency issues before they break your app. Adopt it by adding its script to your CI pipeline, running the scan before each release, and manually fixing any reported problems. It’s production‑ready for prototypes and internal workflows, though you should verify maintenance and documentation before full deployment.

### Русский

Внедрить его можно, добавив в CI pipeline скрипт, который запускает проверку перед каждым релизом, а затем вручную исправляя найденные проблемы.
