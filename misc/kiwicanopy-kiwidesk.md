## 📝 Summary

### English

KiwiDesk is a tiling window manager that feels like it shipped with macOS, yet it’s fully open‑source and configurable. Adopt it by installing via Homebrew, editing the config in ~/.config/kiwidesk, and launching the app; you can hook it into other tools with scripts. It’s production‑ready for prototypes or internal workflows, though you should verify dependencies and maintenance before full deployment.
