> Pre-driver FMLA32 Tensor Core unlocker and PTX benchmark console for GeForce RTX 40-series, RTX 30-series and RTX 20-series on Windows 10/11 (UEFI USB) and Linux (gpu_unlock.ko), with friendly UI for Win/Linux. Restores full 2x FP16/BF16/TF32->FP32 accumulate rate with automatic hardware profiling, pre-flight ROM checks, and zero VBIOS flashing.
