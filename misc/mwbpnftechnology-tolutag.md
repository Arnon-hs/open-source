This project is a passive NFC tag that signs data with ECDSA, with the signature verified on-chain. Adopt it by attaching the tag to a reader, configuring your app to generate signatures, and validating the hash against a smart contract. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, though you should review dependencies and maintenance before production use.
