## 📝 Summary

### English

Scriptorium turns lecture recordings into Obsidian notes and spaced‑repetition flashcards, streamlining study material creation. Adopt it by importing a lecture, configuring templates, and exporting to Obsidian, then using built‑in plugins to generate flashcards. It’s suitable for prototyping, though you should audit dependencies and maintenance before production use.
