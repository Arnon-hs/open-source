To adopt it, simply integrate Boxr into your CI/CD pipeline and swap out Docker commands, ensuring dependencies stay up‑to‑date. Its production readiness is medium—ideal for prototypes and internal workflows, but you should verify maintenance, documentation, and release cadence before deploying it in production.
