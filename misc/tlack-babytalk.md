## 📝 Summary

### English

Babytalk is an offline speech‑to‑text and text‑to‑speech engine for the ESP32, enabling audio processing without internet connectivity. Adoption involves linking the module to an ESP32, configuring the library, and integrating it into your workflow; the project is actively maintained with clear documentation. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal use, though you should verify dependencies and licensing before production deployment.
