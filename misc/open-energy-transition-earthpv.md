## 📝 Summary

### English

Earthpv offers open, global rooftop PV maps derived from free satellite imagery, enabling quick assessment of solar potential worldwide. Adoption involves loading the pre‑generated layers into a GIS platform and performing manual verification of accuracy. The project is suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, though production use requires checking dependency maintenance and support.
