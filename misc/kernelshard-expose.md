## 📝 Summary

### English

Expose is a self‑hosted tunneling tool similar to ngrok that lets you securely publish local services to the internet. To adopt it, simply deploy the container, expose a port, and grab the public URL for use with webhooks or APIs. It’s production‑ready for prototypes and internal workflows, though you should verify repository activity and dependency maintenance before full deployment.
