### English

Cathode is a lightweight Gtk4 YouTube player built on mpv and yt-dlp. To adopt it, install the required dependencies, launch the app, and paste a YouTube URL; the player will fetch and stream the video automatically. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, though you should verify repository activity, licensing, and recent updates before using it in production.
