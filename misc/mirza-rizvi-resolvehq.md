ResolveHQ is a helpdesk built on Cloudflare Workers, D1, R2, and queues, enabling rapid deployment of support services with minimal setup. To adopt, deploy the repo on Cloudflare, configure D1 tables and R2 storage for your data, and manually review any external integrations. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but production use requires checking activity, documentation, and licensing.
