### English

Novgraph is a knowledge‑graph engine for codebases that lets you quickly map dependencies and contextual relationships within your code. To adopt it, add the package to your project, run the graph‑generation script, and hook it into your IDE or CI/CD pipeline. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, though you should audit dependencies and maintenance before deploying to production.
