## 📝 Summary

### English

Toolcall-doctor is a tool that shrinks and simplifies broken LLM tool‑call reproducers, helping developers quickly identify and fix invocation issues. Adopt it by manually validating compatibility with your stack, then hook it into your CI/CD pipeline to automatically test calls. It’s production‑ready for prototypes and internal workflows, though you should audit dependencies and maintenance before full deployment.
