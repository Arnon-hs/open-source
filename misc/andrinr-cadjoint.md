## 📝 Summary

### English

cadjoint is a browser‑based library for differentiable implicit CAD, enabling rapid prototyping of geometric models with derivative support. To adopt it, simply add the npm package to your web app and call its API, but first verify dependencies and documentation. The project is suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, though a production rollout should include a license and maintenance review.
