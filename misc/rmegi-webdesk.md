Webdesk is a browser‑based Linux desktop delivered over SSH, enabling quick deployment of a full desktop environment without installing an OS. Start by pulling the Docker image, SSH‑into your server, and open Webdesk in a browser—setup takes just minutes. It’s production‑ready for prototypes and internal workflows, though you should verify dependency maintenance and support before scaling.
