Levenshtein provides a curated set of Go lint rules and CI checks to keep code clean and consistent. Adopt it by adding the rules to your linter (e.g., golangci‑lint) and configuring your CI pipeline, then run a quick compatibility scan on your repo. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but verify maintenance, licensing, and documentation before production use.
