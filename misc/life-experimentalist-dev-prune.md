### English

Dev-prune removes node_modules only when the lockfile can rebuild them, saving space and speeding up builds. Start by adding it to your CI pipeline, ensuring the lockfile is up-to-date, and include it in your cleanup script. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, though a manual review is advised before production use.
