### English

Weft provides red squiggle visualizations that help teams of coding agents track interactions between tasks. Adopt it by integrating the repo into your CI/CD pipeline and configuring display rules, giving the team instant feedback on conflict areas. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but you should audit dependencies and maintenance before production use.
