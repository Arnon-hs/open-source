## 📝 Summary

### English

Dataloupe turns any CSV, Parquet, or Excel file into an offline HTML explorer, making data browsing and analysis effortless without an internet connection. To adopt it, simply download the package, place your files in the project folder, and open index.html in a browser. It’s ready for prototypes and internal workflows, though you should verify dependency health and maintenance before production use.
