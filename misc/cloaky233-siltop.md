## 📝 Summary

### English

Siltop is a lightweight system monitor designed for Macs with Apple Silicon, filling the gap left by btop’s lack of support. Install it via Homebrew, tweak the config to your workflow, and launch; it instantly displays CPU, memory, and network metrics. It’s ready for prototyping and internal use, though you should verify dependency health and maintenance before deploying to production.

Чтобы начать использовать, установите его через Homebrew, настройте конфигурацию под свои нужды и запустите; он быстро покажет метрики CPU, памяти и сети.
