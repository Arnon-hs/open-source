PS5LM brings Llama.cpp and any GGUF model to run natively on a jailbroken PS5, delivering high performance without emulation. Adoption involves installing the jailbreak patch, copying the model to the console’s storage, and launching the provided script; it’s production‑ready for prototypes but needs dependency checks before full deployment.
