Conecta enables blue‑green deployments on a VPS by leveraging DNS routing and Caddy as the web server. To adopt it, simply configure a Caddyfile and DNS records, then switch traffic between versions with zero downtime. It is suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, though dependencies and maintenance should be verified before production use.
