## 📝 Summary

### English

Bothy is a one‑command terminal workspace that stitches together trusted tools you already use. To adopt, install the package, run `bothy init`, and it will scaffold a workspace with your preferred editors and utilities. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but verify dependency health and maintenance before production use.
