## 📝 Summary

### English

Proxion is an open‑source web console for Proxmox VE that lets you manage VMs directly from a browser without installing client software. To adopt it, simply deploy the official Docker image and point it at your Proxmox cluster; the UI will auto‑discover nodes and resources. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but before production use, verify dependency health and ongoing maintenance.
