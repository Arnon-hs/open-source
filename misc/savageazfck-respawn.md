## 📝 Summary

### English

Respawn is a content‑addressed filesystem that lets you take snapshots and undo changes. Adopt it by adding the crate to your Rust project, calling the snapshot API before critical operations and rolling back when needed. It’s production‑ready for prototypes or internal workflows, though you should audit dependencies and maintenance before full deployment.
