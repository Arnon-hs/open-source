## 📝 Summary

### English

SPEAR is a C++‑based simulator that lets researchers quickly add photorealistic embodied AI capabilities to their projects without building a full model stack from scratch. It is best suited for prototyping AI features, RAG or agent workflows, and evaluating model tooling, but requires manual inspection and setup validation before adoption due to sparse integration signals. While useful for internal prototypes, production deployment should follow dependency and maintenance checks, reflecting its medium readiness level.
