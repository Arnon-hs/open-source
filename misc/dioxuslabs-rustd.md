## 📝 Summary

### English

Rustd is a drop‑in replacement for the Rust compiler (rustc) that aims to accelerate build times without changing code. Adoption is as simple as replacing `rustc` with `rustd` in your build scripts, after a quick verification of compatibility. It is medium‑ready for prototypes or internal use, but requires license, maintenance, documentation and release‑cadence checks before production.
