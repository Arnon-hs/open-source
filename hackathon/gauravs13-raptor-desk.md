> Self-hosted hackathon portal built for DOGFOOD 2026: submissions, fair judging and a public vote. Corrects judge bias, flags close calls and   kingmaker judges, asks the right judges, records decisions with reasons, and publishes signed results anyone can verify offline. T1–T4   verified by both checkers. One command: docker compose up.
