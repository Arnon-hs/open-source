## 📝 Summary

### English

Fouine is a local full‑text and semantic search tool for scanned PDFs on macOS that makes internal knowledge bases instantly searchable for assistants. To adopt it, index your PDFs and hook the index into your assistant via the provided API. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but production use should confirm maintenance status and dependency compatibility.
