> Star if Papyra keeps your notes yours! A self-hosted, Google Keep-style notes app where every note is a plain Markdown file on your disk. Live collaboration, passkeys, encrypted locked notes, full-text search and one-container Docker setup. Crafted with 💗 for people who want their notes without lock-in.
