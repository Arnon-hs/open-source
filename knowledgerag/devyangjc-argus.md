### English

**Project Summary**

Argus is an open-source knowledge library platform built on the RAG architecture, utilizing Java 21, Spring Boot, and PostgreSQL/pgvector for backend development, and Vue 3, TypeScript, and Element Plus for frontend development. The AI layer is powered by Spring AI Alibaba and ReactAgent, with MinIO and Elasticsearch serving as storage and retrieval engines. This platform enables internal knowledge to be searchable and usable by assistants.

**Value Proposition**

The value proposition of Argus lies in its ability to make internal knowledge searchable and usable by assistants, thereby improving the efficiency and accuracy of knowledge retrieval and application. This is particularly useful for organizations with large knowledge bases that need to be indexed and searched efficiently.
