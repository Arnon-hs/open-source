### English

**Project Summary:**

green-dalii/obsidian-llm-wiki is an open-source plugin for Obsidian, a note-taking app, that turns notes and PDFs into a linked, AI-powered knowledge base. This implementation of Karpathy's LLM Wiki provides entity pages, concept pages, and graph-powered Q&A, making internal knowledge searchable and usable by assistants. With its strong ecosystem signals and high production readiness, this plugin is suitable for serious pilots.

**Value Proposition:**

The primary value proposition of green-dalii/obsidian-llm-wiki lies in its ability to make internal knowledge searchable and usable by assistants. By indexing knowledge bases, improving search over documents, and grounding assistant answers, this plugin helps organizations streamline their knowledge management and improve the productivity of their assistants.

**Practical Adoption Path:**

To adopt green-dalii/obsidian-llm-wiki, users can follow these steps:

1. Install the plugin in Obsidian.
2. Configure the plugin to index notes and PDFs.
3. Explore the linked, AI-powered knowledge base.
4. Use the graph-powered Q&A feature to query the knowledge base.
