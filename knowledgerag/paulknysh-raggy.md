## 📝 Summary

### English

Raggy is a lightweight CLI tool for RAG over local documents that lets you quickly index internal knowledge bases, enhance document search, and ground assistant answers with data. Adopt it by cloning the repo, installing dependencies, running the index command, and hooking the output into your assistant via API. It’s production‑ready for prototypes and internal workflows, though you should verify dependency health and maintenance before full deployment.
