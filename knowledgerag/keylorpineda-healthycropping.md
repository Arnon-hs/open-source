HealthyCropping combines offline Android crop-disease diagnosis using TFLite with RAG, making internal knowledge easier to search and helping assistants ground their answers. Start with a small pilot using real documents and field images, measuring diagnostic accuracy, retrieval quality, and integration effort; its production readiness is medium, so verify licensing, dependencies, documentation, issues, and release cadence before deployment.
