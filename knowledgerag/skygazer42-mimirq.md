### English

**Project Summary**

MimirQ is an open-source enterprise RAG (Retriever Augmented Generator) knowledge library that prioritizes Chinese support. It offers controlled parsing, governance, chunking, hybrid search, re-ranking, citation, and integration with GraphRAG and Dify. By making internal knowledge searchable and usable, MimirQ helps organizations improve their search capabilities and ground assistant answers.

**Value Proposition**

MimirQ provides significant value to organizations by:

1. **Improving search capabilities**: MimirQ enables controlled parsing and hybrid search, allowing users to find relevant information more efficiently.
2. **Grounding assistant answers**: By indexing knowledge bases, MimirQ helps ensure that assistant answers are accurate and reliable.
3. **Enhancing knowledge management**: MimirQ's governance features enable organizations to manage their knowledge bases effectively.

**Practical Adoption Path**

To adopt MimirQ, organizations can follow these steps:

1. **Evaluate the project**: Review MimirQ's documentation, code, and community activity to ensure it meets their needs.
2. **Integrate with existing systems**: Use MimirQ's API/SDK/CLI to integrate with existing knowledge management systems and assistants.
3. **Configure and customize**: Set up MimirQ to meet specific organizational requirements
