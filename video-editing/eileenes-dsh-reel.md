## 📝 Summary

### English

Eileenes/dsh-reel lets you quickly produce promotional reels and thumbnails for DSH plugins, rendering frames locally with an integrated renderer. Simply install via npm and run the command to generate a 28‑second video and cover image, all without extra runtime. It’s suitable for prototyping and internal workflows, though you should audit dependencies and maintenance before production use.
