## 📝 Summary

### English

open-ribbi/velocut is an AI‑native, local‑first video editor that runs entirely in the browser, enabling automated editing, subtitle generation, and rendering through a unified JSON command protocol for both humans and LLM agents. It’s ideal for prototypes and internal workflows, though production use requires a review of dependencies and maintainer activity.
