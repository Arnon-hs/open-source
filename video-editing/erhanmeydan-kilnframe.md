### English

Kilnframe is a free, open-source plugin for Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve that turns spoken input into animated inserts and music while rendering on your own computer. Start with a small test project and a repeatable pipeline for captions, dubbing, editing, and rendering; before production use, verify licensing, dependencies, security, and maintainer activity, as its readiness is currently assessed as medium.
