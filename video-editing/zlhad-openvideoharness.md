## 📝 Summary

### English

ZLHad/OpenVideoHarness is a video harness platform that lets agents like Claude or Codex write code to render, check frames, storyboard, and cut films. With preset styles, rich engines, and code‑based audio/visual editing, it enables rapid prototyping and final approval of videos. The project is production‑ready, recently updated, Python‑based, and has strong community signals for a serious pilot.
