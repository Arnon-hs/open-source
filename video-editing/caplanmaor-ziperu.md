### English

Ziperu is rubyzip with a Rust core, keeping the same API but faster and safer. It enables automated media editing, rendering, and transcoding, as well as subtitle and dubbing generation, turning workflows into repeatable pipelines. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but you should verify support, documentation, and updates before production use.
