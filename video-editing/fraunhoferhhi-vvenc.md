## 📝 Summary

### English

This project lets you quickly generate your first VVC clip with vvenc 1.14 and observe how modern browsers refuse to play it, making it an excellent educational resource. Start by installing vvenc, encoding a test file, and testing it in a browser, then integrate the workflow into your automated video processing pipeline. It’s production‑ready for prototypes and internal workflows, though you should verify dependencies and maintenance before full deployment.
