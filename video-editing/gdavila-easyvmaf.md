## 📝 Summary

### English

This project offers a step‑by‑step guide to using libvmaf_cuda on Windows via WSL2, Docker, and NVIDIA, streamlining the integration of video quality assessment into workflows. A practical adoption path starts with installing WSL2 and Docker, then running a GPU‑enabled container and executing VMAF tests. It is suitable for prototypes and internal pipelines, though dependency and maintenance checks are needed before production use.
