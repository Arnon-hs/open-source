## 📝 Summary

### English

Apache Hive is an open‑source data‑warehouse framework that translates SQL‑like queries into scalable MapReduce, Tez or Spark jobs, letting organizations turn raw, Hadoop‑stored data into searchable tables and automated analytics pipelines. Its strong community (≈6 k stars, frequent releases, and wide ecosystem adoption) makes it production‑ready for pilots, though the integration steps aren’t obvious from the repo metadata and should start with a small proof‑of‑concept that follows the README and validates cluster setup and connector configuration. By first wiring a limited dataset through Hive’s metastore and execution engine, teams can assess operational costs before scaling the solution to broader reporting and pipeline use cases.
