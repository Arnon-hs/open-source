> FlowOutside is an open-source productivity web application built for the Hacktoberfest Touch Grass Challenge. It solves screen burnout by taking a messy, unorganized to-do list and transforming it into a structured, alternating schedule—interleaving indoor focus sprints with optimized real-world outdoor errands and nature breaks.
