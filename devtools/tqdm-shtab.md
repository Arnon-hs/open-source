By adding a few lines to a project’s setup (e.g., calling `shtab.complete` in the CLI entry point), teams can instantly enable Bash, Zsh, or Fish completions, accelerating daily workflows and CI feedback loops. The library is actively maintained, recently updated, and shows strong adoption signals—making it a production‑ready choice for a pilot or broader rollout.
