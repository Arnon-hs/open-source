## 📝 Summary

### English

**Project Summary:**

Omadia is an open-source, self-hostable agentic OS that enables users to build, run, and audit multi-agent AI teams from signed plugins. This platform empowers users to bring their own Large Language Model (LLM) keys, own all their data, and adhere to EU/GDPR regulations. Omadia helps integrate isolated tools and prompts into repeatable agent workflows, making it an attractive solution for coordinating complex tasks.

**Value Proposition:**

Omadia offers a unique value proposition by allowing users to standardize agent memory, add tool-use pipelines, and coordinate multi-agent workflows. This enables users to streamline their AI operations, increase efficiency, and reduce costs.
