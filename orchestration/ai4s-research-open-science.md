**Project Summary:**

Open Science is an open-source, local-first AI workbench designed for scientists, providing a model-agnostic and reproducible research desktop for macOS and Windows. This project helps transform isolated prompts and tools into repeatable agent workflows, enabling scientists to coordinate multi-agent workflows, standardize agent memory, and add tool-use pipelines. With its straightforward integration and recent activity, Open Science is a promising alternative to Claude Science.

The primary value of Open Science lies in its ability to turn isolated prompts and tools into repeatable agent workflows, making it easier for scientists to collaborate and reproduce results.
