4. **Scale** – Once the pilot is stable, adopt the patterns across multiple agents, standardize the memory and tool‑use conventions, and incorporate the site as an internal developer portal.

- **Suitability:** Ideal for prototypes, internal tooling, or as a learning hub; not yet a drop‑in production component.  
- **Risks:** Integration steps are not explicit; you’ll need to translate the tutorial snippets into your language/framework and verify dependencies (e.g., LangChain, OpenAI SDK).
