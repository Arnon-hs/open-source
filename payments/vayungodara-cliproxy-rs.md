## 📝 Summary

### English

Cliproxy-rs is a Rust port of the CLIProxyAPI that enables rapid integration of monetization, billing, and PSP flows into your applications. Start by using the ready‑made client, configuring subscription parameters, and hooking it into your existing payment system. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but production use requires verifying dependency health and ongoing maintenance.
