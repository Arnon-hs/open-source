**Project Summary**

EasyMetaAu/helm-api is an open-source, self-hosted LLM (Large Language Model) gateway that enables routing traffic from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini through a configuration file, rather than coding. This project offers a valuable solution for adding AI capabilities without starting from scratch, ideal for prototyping AI features, building RAG (Reusable AI Gateway) or agent workflows, and evaluating model tooling. With its straightforward integration and medium production readiness, it's a viable option for internal workflows and development environments.

**Value Proposition**

The primary value of EasyMetaAu/helm-api lies in its ability to simplify the process of integrating AI capabilities into existing systems. By providing a configuration-based routing system, developers can easily switch between different LLM providers and experiment with various models without requiring extensive coding or infrastructure changes. This flexibility and ease of use make it an attractive solution for development teams looking to add AI capabilities to their applications.
