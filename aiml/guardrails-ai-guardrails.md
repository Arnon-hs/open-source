### English

Guardrails is an open-source project that adds AI capabilities to large language models without requiring a complete model stack from scratch. Its value proposition lies in enabling the rapid prototyping of AI features and the evaluation of model tooling, making it an attractive solution for developers looking to build and refine their AI-powered applications. With its strong adoption, recent activity, and high production readiness, Guardrails is a viable candidate for serious pilots and production use cases, although a thorough review of its license, security posture, and maintainers is still necessary.
