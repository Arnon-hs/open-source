## 📝 Summary

### English

**Project Summary:**
Lintlang is an open-source static linter for AI configurations, tool descriptions, and system prompts that ensures quality and consistency before runtime. It detects language-level failures, such as vague tool descriptions and missing stop conditions, using deterministic regex and structural detectors without relying on machine learning models. This tool facilitates the addition of AI capabilities without starting from scratch.

**Value Proposition:**
Lintlang offers significant value to developers and organizations looking to integrate AI capabilities into their systems. By catching errors and inconsistencies early, it saves time and resources, while ensuring that AI-powered workflows are reliable and efficient. This tool is particularly useful for prototyping AI features, building Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) or agent workflows, and evaluating model tooling.

**Practical Adoption Path:**
To integrate Lintlang into your development workflow, start by evaluating its feasibility through a small proof of concept. Review the README documentation and ensure that the tool aligns with your project's requirements. Once you've validated its usefulness, you can incorporate Lintlang into your continuous integration (CI) pipeline to catch language-level failures before runtime.
