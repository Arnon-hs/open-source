### English

Here's a brief summary of the SandboxFleet project:

SandboxFleet is an open-source project that enables the creation of multiple isolated AI agent sandboxes on shared workers, streamlining the development and testing of AI features. By leveraging this tool, developers can quickly prototype AI capabilities without starting from scratch, making it an ideal solution for building proof-of-concepts, RAG (Reinforcement Agent Graph) workflows, and evaluating model tooling. While it's suitable for internal workflows and prototypes, its production readiness is medium due to potential dependency and maintenance issues that require careful evaluation before deployment.
