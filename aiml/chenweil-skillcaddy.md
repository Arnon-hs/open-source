## 📝 Summary

### English

**Project Summary:**

SkillCaddy is an open-source, local AI skills central library that enables per-project symlink enablement. This allows developers to easily share AI capabilities across various projects, such as Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, and Pi. By using SkillCaddy, developers can add AI capability without starting from a blank model stack, making it ideal for prototyping AI features and building RAG or agent workflows.

**Value:**

The primary value proposition of SkillCaddy lies in its ability to simplify the integration of AI capabilities across multiple projects. By providing a central library of AI skills, developers can easily share and reuse code, reducing the time and effort required to build and deploy AI-powered applications.

**Practical Adoption Path:**

To adopt SkillCaddy, developers can follow these steps:

1. Install the SkillCaddy library in their project.
2. Create a symlink to the desired AI skill from the central library into the project's `.agents/skills` or `.claude/skills` directory.
3. Use the shared AI skill in the project.
