> PagePilot is a local-first, privacy-focused browser assistant that runs entirely on your machine. Powered by Ollama and the open-weight Gemma 3 4B model, it reads your active tabs and answers questions without ever sending your personal data to the cloud. Built for Hacktoberfest 2026.
