> Kill the process on any port (3000, 5173, 8080) with one command: npx kill-port-safe 3000. Fixes EADDRINUSE "address already in use" on Windows, macOS, Linux & Docker. Zero-dependency drop-in replacement for kill-port: exact port matching, shows what it killed, graceful shutdown, CI-safe exit codes, TypeScript + ESM.
