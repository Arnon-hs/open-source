> Make any inline SVG draw itself. Strokes animate on one by one, then the fills and labels fade in. Scrub to any point, or record the whole thing to a WebM clip without a server.  Built on vivus [(https://github.com/maxwellito/vivus). extended for real-world SVGs containing strokes, fills, and text.
