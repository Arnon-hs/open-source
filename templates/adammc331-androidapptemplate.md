3. **Security & licensing audit:** Review the repository’s LICENSE file, run a dependency scanner (e.g., OWASP Dependency‑Check), and confirm no prohibited third‑party code.  
4. **Gradual rollout:** Adopt the template for a new internal module or a low‑risk prototype, then iterate based on feedback before scaling to larger teams or public releases.
