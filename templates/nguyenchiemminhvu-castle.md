## 📝 Summary

### English

Castle 2.0 is a lightweight template library for embedded systems that lets teams quickly reuse working workflows and production presets. Adopt it by importing the desired templates into your project, configuring them for your target microcontroller, and validating on a prototype. It’s suitable for prototypes and internal workflows, but production use should be preceded by a review of dependencies, maintenance status, and documentation.

Чтобы начать, импортируйте нужные шаблоны в свой проект, настройте параметры под конкретный микроконтроллер и протестируйте на прототипе.
